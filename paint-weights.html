Paint weights in Blender

Paint a piece's weights in Blender from one button, close a seam between two pieces, and see which weights a save cut.

How-to4 min readTimbre 26.1106.0 · Overtone 26.1106.0

Weights say which bones move each vertex. Overtone's Weights panel takes the chores out of painting them:

  • One button to start. Paint weights readies the piece, Rig and X mirror, four settings you once set by hand.
  • Seams that hold. Match seam gives a piece what it touches on the next one, so both bend alike.
  • No jump found in game. A save tells you which pieces lost a weight, and Select them finds the vertices.

#Before you start

  • The mod is open in Blender from Timbre (Open a mod and save it). A Blender you started yourself paints, but cannot match a seam or find a cut weight.
  • The sidebar is open: press N and open the Overtone tab.

#Your first stroke

  1. Select a piece.
  2. Under Weights, press Paint weights. The piece shows its weights in colour, and the panel says Groups 18 in use.
  3. Alt+click a bone of Rig. The piece shows that bone's weights.
  4. Paint on the piece. The other side of it follows.
  5. Press Ctrl+S. The save is taken, and you are still painting.
  6. Press Stop painting. The piece and Rig are back in Object Mode.

#What a piece lists, and what a save does

Paint weights drops the groups no vertex of the piece weighs on and sorts the rest down the body, so the list shows what the piece uses. Nothing else changes a group: a save changes no weight you paint, and writes the mod at rest, whatever the pose.

The game reads four or eight weights a vertex, as many as the character has. A save keeps the strongest of a vertex's weights and drops the rest.

#Paint what you want

You want Do this
To paint a bone with no group on this piece Alt+click it and paint: your first stroke makes its group
To see a bend as you paint Pose Rig
To paint one side only Turn off X at the top of the view
Alt to move the window, not click a bone Ctrl+Shift+click the bone instead

A vertex with no twin across the middle paints on its own side.

#Close a seam between two pieces

Two pieces with unlike weights part when a bone turns. Match seam gives the piece you pick the weights of what it touches.

  1. Select the piece that should change.
  2. Press Tab for Edit Mode.
  3. Select the vertices along the seam.
  4. Under Weights, press Match seam. The status bar says Matched 54 vertices, 120 behind them.
  5. Pose Rig and check the seam.

The other piece keeps its weights. A hard line beside the seam would crease in a pose, so the rows behind it are blended in: change Rings under Adjust Last Operation to widen or narrow them, and see it change. Ctrl+Z takes the whole press back. It works in Weight Paint too, with vertex selection on.

Match seam is not enough for every seam:

The seam What it does Do this
Two of the game's own pieces Makes them alike, but the game hides these by overlap and they part in a pose anyway Leave them. Match a piece you made or brought in
Lit differently on each side Changes no normal Use Blender's Data Transfer on Custom Normals
Lies on a second layer, such as a hem over a thigh Takes the surface facing it, and may take the wrong one Check it in a pose, and paint what is wrong
Runs along several pieces Changes the active piece alone Press it on each piece that should change

#Find the weights a save cut

Past the game's limit, the status bar says so after a save: model.glb taken; 1,204 vertices of Skirt, Belt lost a weight past 8. A weight too small to move a vertex is not counted.

  1. Select a piece the status bar named. The panel says Past 8 weights 1,204 vertices.
  2. Under Weights, press Select them. Those vertices are selected.
  3. In Weight Paint, repaint them, or press Weights → Limit Total and set Limit to the number the message gave.
  4. Press Ctrl+S. The status bar no longer names the piece.
You see Do this
A few vertices named, and no jump in a pose Nothing. The save still takes the piece
No vertex holds too many weights now Press Ctrl+S: the count is from your last save
Start Blender from Timbre to find them Open the mod from Timbre

#What you can't do yet

  • Match several pieces at once. Match seam changes the active piece.
  • Keep a group locked through a match. Match seam writes through Blender's locks.
  • Have a save fix weights past the limit. It tells you, and the painting is yours.

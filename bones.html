Bone names

What a bone's name says in Blender and everywhere else: every word a bone can have, what ~ in front of a name means, and how a WWMI number finds its bone.

Reference6 min readTimbre 26.1106.0 · Overtone 26.1106.0

A bone's name says what the bone is. You read it on Rig in the Blender scene, and on each vertex group of a piece. Overtone ships a skeleton table of every character and reads each name from it, so two mods of one character and a dump of her call a bone the same. You do not edit a name: the game reads a bone's number, never its name.

#How a name is built

Form Example Used for
A word and a side thigh.L Body bones
A word, a number and a side forearm twist 2.L, index 2.R Twists and fingers
A joint, a place and a side knee back.L Helpers
A group, a chain, a bone and a side hair 3.2.L Chains

.L is the character's left and .R her right. A name with no side lies on her middle, such as head. Blender's own mirror tools read these sides, so a bone finds its image by name.

A second number before the side, as in upper arm twist 1 2.L, tells apart two bones that would otherwise share one name.

Rig sorts its bones into collections by their first word: Body, Fingers, Face, Hair, Skirt and Cloth.

#Body

These are in the Body collection, top to bottom.

Word The bone
head The head.
neck The neck. Where it has two bones, neck 1 and neck 2.
chest The upper chest, where the arms and neck hang from.
spine 1, spine 2 The spine, counted up from the hips.
hips The root of the body. A guess may call it pelvis.
clavicle The collarbone.
upper arm Shoulder to elbow.
forearm Elbow to wrist.
hand The hand.
thigh Hip to knee.
calf Knee to ankle.
foot The foot.
toe The toes, as one bone.

Example: calf.L hangs from thigh.L and carries foot.L.

#Twists

A twist bone turns with a limb along its length, so the skin does not pinch as the limb rolls. They are in the Body collection, and are named <limb> twist <number>, counted down the limb.

Word Example
upper arm twist upper arm twist 1.L
forearm twist forearm twist 2.R
thigh twist thigh twist 1.L
calf twist calf twist 1.R

#Helpers

A helper is a small bone beside a joint, which holds the skin in shape as the joint bends. They are in the Body collection, and are named <joint> <place>.

Joint Between
hip thigh and the hips
knee calf and thigh
ankle foot and calf
shoulder upper arm and the chest
elbow forearm and upper arm
wrist hand and forearm
A joint has Its helpers are named
Two front and back
Three front, mid and back
One, or more than three A number: hip helper 1.L

front lies ahead of the joint, the way the character faces, and back behind it: knee front is over the kneecap and knee back in the hollow behind it. hip mid lies on the hip's outer side.

#Fingers

These are in the Fingers collection, and are named <finger> <bone>. A finger's words are thumb, index, middle, ring and pinky. Its bones count out from the hand: index 1.L is nearest the palm and index 3.L is the tip.

Two words come only from a guess (Marks): palm 1.L, a bone in the palm, and finger 6.1.R, a sixth finger's first bone.

#Chains

A chain is a line of bones that hangs from the body and swings, or a line of the face's small bones. Each is named <group> <chain>.<bone>, with a side where it has one.

Group Collection Holds
face Face The face's small bones.
hair Hair Hair.
skirt Skirt Skirts.
cloth Cloth Other cloth.

In hair 3.2.L, 3 is the chain and 2 its second bone. Bone 1 is nearest the body, and the numbers grow outward.

  • A chain and its image share a number. hair 3.1.L and hair 3.1.R mirror each other.
  • A whole chain has one side. Its first bone decides it. A chain with no image keeps that side, so a .L chain need not have a .R.
  • A branch is a chain of its own. Its first bone hangs from a bone of the chain it leaves.
  • A chain's number means one bone on one character. hair 3.2.L on another character is another bone. Only the group says the same thing on both.

#Marks

On Rig Means Do this
thigh.L The table proves this bone. Use it.
~thigh.L A guess. Overtone worked the name out, and the table does not prove it, usually for a mod made for an older game version or a character the table does not hold yet. Use it, and pose Rig to check it.
c3.b17 Nothing named this bone. It is number 17 of part 3. Pose Rig to see what it moves.
thigh.L own A bone of yours held a name the model wants, so Overtone moved yours aside. Rename it, or delete it.

A bone marked ~ saves and draws like any other. A vertex group named thigh.L on a mesh you bring in counts as ~thigh.L.

#WWMI numbers

WWMI-Tools calls a bone by a number within a Component, and Component 3 is Overtone's Part 3 (Coming from WWMI-Tools). A number means a bone only with its part: number 17 is one bone in Part 3 and another in Part 1. There is no number for the whole mesh. A game update can move the numbers, and the name stays with the bone.

The Weights panel, under Overtone in the sidebar of a Blender opened from Timbre, shows the bone a number is. Press N to open the sidebar. With a piece selected it speaks of the active vertex group's bone, and with Rig selected of the active bone:

upper arm.L
Part 3 · 17
Also Part 1 · 4

The first line is the name, the second the bone's number in its own part, and Also lists every other part that holds it.

To find a bone by its WWMI number:

  1. Click a piece in the part the number belongs to. The panel shows that piece's active vertex group.
  2. Type the number into Find number and press Enter. The panel shows the bone's name and Part 3 · 17, and that bone's vertex group becomes the active one.
The panel says Means
No bone holds that number here That part has no such number. Check you clicked a piece of the right part.
The same bone as before The bone is active on Rig, but this piece has no weights on it, so it has no vertex group to show.
Select a piece or a bone Neither a piece nor Rig is active.
No active bone Rig has no active bone, or a piece's active vertex group names no bone.

With Rig selected, Find number reads the number in the part of the active bone.

#When a name changes

You see Why
A bone you renamed has its old name back Each open names every bone as the model does. Its vertex groups follow.
A dump's names do not match this page A dump keeps the names it was made with. Dump the character again (How to create a mod).
A name lost its ~, or c3.b17 became a word An Overtone update's table now holds the bone.

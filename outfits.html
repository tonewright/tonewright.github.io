Mix outfits and presets

Wear several mods on one character, take pieces from different outfits, and save what you wear as presets you can put back on in game.

Words used here:

Word Means
outfit A mod you turn on for a character, with any mods inside its folder
part One section of the character the game draws: hair, face, body and so on
piece One object of an outfit, such as a jacket or a hat, named by its author
preset A saved set of the outfits you wear, with their switches

#Two outfits at once

You can wear as many outfits on a character as you like. What happens depends on the parts they change.

  • Different parts draw together. A face mod with a costume, or a hat with a body suit: both draw, each on its own parts.
  • A shared part draws one outfit. Two outfits that both change her body never draw over each other. One draws, and the other draws nothing anywhere on her.

When you turn an outfit on in Timbre that shares a part with one already on, Timbre turns the other off and says Turning it on put away 1 mod it would have clashed with. Press Keep both to leave both on: then one draws and the other waits.

The Loadout page in Timbre names each outfit that waits, as <outfit> draws; <other> is passed over, with a Wear <other> button to draw it instead. Timbre's In game panel marks the one waiting Passed over for <outfit>.

To wear pieces of both, mix them.

#Mix pieces of several outfits

A mix takes pieces from any of a character's outfits: one outfit's jacket, another's skirt, a third's hat.

#In Timbre

  1. Open the character, and press Mix outfits, the button beside the globe at the top. Or select two or more of her mods and press Mix in the selection bar.
  2. On the left, under Add to mix, add the outfits you want pieces from.
  3. On the right, each part lists the pieces the outfits draw there. Tick the ones to wear. Pointing at a piece names it on the model in the middle, and lights it in game while the game runs.
  4. Press Wear mix. The bar says which preset it Saves to.

Each part's Part options menu also offers:

  • Original: the game's own part there.
  • Hide part: nothing there.
  • Use pieces: the pieces again.

A card warns Two outfits here where two outfits draw on one body or face part, with Keep for one of them, and Nothing here where an outfit hides a part no piece covers, with Show original.

While she wears a mix, her page says Mix: with its outfits, and offers Edit mix and Un-mix. Un-mix wears each outfit whole again. Undo takes back a mix or an un-mix.

#In game

  1. Press Home, open Wardrobe, and switch to its Mix page (Ctrl+PgDn from Outfits). Q and E step through characters.
  2. Each part shows the pieces worn there in their outfits' colours, or Original, Hidden or Removed.
  3. Open a part (Enter) to switch its pieces. A piece of an outfit she doesn't wear puts that outfit on with that piece alone; Space puts it on with every piece. + Add puts on another outfit.
  4. Every change shows on her at once and is only tried on. Press Keep (/) to keep it, or Revert (Delete) to go back. Leaving the page also goes back.

#What a mix can't do

  • A switch still decides when a piece shows: a piece its outfit's switch hides stays hidden.
  • Pieces of one costume don't go on another costume's model.
  • Pieces that pass through each other are not detected: check her from every side.

#Presets

A preset saves which outfits are on, each with its switches. Putting one back on sets them all again.

  • Default is always there, in wheel slot 1: what you wore when it was made. You can save over it, but not rename or delete it.
  • Changing what you wear doesn't change a preset. The preset shows as <name> (modified) until you save it again, and Revert puts it back as saved.
  • A preset holds at least one mod: with nothing on, there is nothing to save. F8 turns every mod off instead.
  • A preset that names a mod you deleted puts back the rest, says so once, and forgets that mod.

#In Timbre

On the Loadout page:

  • + saves what is on now as a preset; name it and press Save. A name already used replaces that preset.
  • Pick a preset from the bar's list to put it on. If what you wear is not saved, Timbre asks first; Undo switches the mods back.
  • Rename or delete the loaded preset, beside the list, also sets its Wheel slot.

#In game

Press Home and open Presets. It shows the wheel's eight slots.

To Press
Choose a slot The arrow keys, or click it
Wear the slot's preset Wear, Enter or Space
Save what you wear into the slot Save what I wear here, or Ctrl+S; twice where a preset is there already
Take a preset off the wheel Take off the wheel, or Delete
Put a preset on the wheel Ctrl+click its chip under the wheel

#The preset wheel

Hold a key to show the wheel over the game, point at a preset and let go to wear it. Letting go without pointing wears nothing.

The wheel has no key until you give it one: in the overlay, open Settings → Keys and set Preset wheel, then press Save. Timbre's Settings → Runtime → Keys has the same setting.

Applies to Timbre 26.1017.0 and Overtone 26.1017.0.

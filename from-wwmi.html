Coming from WWMI

What changes when you move from WWMI and the XXMI Launcher to Overtone and Timbre, what carries over, and how to keep both.

Your WWMI mods work in Overtone as they are: Timbre reads their ini files once and converts them. You don't need to change a mod, and you don't need the XXMI Launcher.

#What is different

WWMI Overtone
Graphics DirectX 11 DirectX 12, the game's default
Started by The XXMI Launcher The game's own launcher, or Timbre's Play
A mod is read From its ini files, every time it loads From its description, overtone.mod.toml, which Timbre writes from the ini files
Turning a mod off Renaming its folder DISABLED… A switch in Timbre or the overlay; folder names mean nothing
A mod's switches Its keys, and a menu some mods draw Its keys, and a row per switch in the overlay's Wardrobe and in Timbre
Saved choices d3dx_user.ini Each mod's .overtone/state.toml
Several outfits All draw, one over the other where they share a part Mods on different parts draw together; on a shared part, one draws and you choose which (Mix outfits and presets)
After a game update Mods may break until their authors fix them Mods keep working when the update renumbers bones; a changed model still needs the author's update

#What carries over

  • Your mods. Add them through Timbre (Add your mods). Mods whose folder starts with DISABLED come in turned off.
  • Their keys. A mod's keys are the ones its author gave it. F10 still reloads mods.

#What does not carry over

  • Your choices in d3dx_user.ini. Every mod starts at its author's defaults. Set them again in the overlay's Wardrobe, then save them as a preset so they survive.
  • A mod's own in-game menu. Overtone does not draw it. Its choices are switches in the Wardrobe instead.
  • Glow maps, and some hair light maps and highlights, which keep the game's. Overtone does not draw these yet.

#Things that work differently

  • A mod's keys work only while it is on screen. With two mods on screen on the same key, the press goes to the one drawn most recently. Switching from the Wardrobe never clashes.
  • Converted mods no longer load in WWMI. Timbre moves a mod's ini files into its .overtone/legacy/ folder when it converts it (what native means). Nothing is deleted.
  • Two outfits on one part don't draw over each other. Only one draws there. Turning one on in Timbre turns off the one it clashes with, and offers Keep both (Mix outfits and presets).
  • Choices are kept per mod, in its own folder, so moving a mod keeps them.

#Keep WWMI too

You can keep using WWMI for comparison, as long as each has its own folder of mods.

  1. Leave your XXMI Mods folder as it is.
  2. Give Timbre a copy (Add your mods), never XXMI's folder itself.
  3. To play with WWMI, start the game from the XXMI Launcher. To play with Overtone, start it from Timbre's Play or the game's launcher on DirectX 12.

Overtone stays in the game's folder and stands aside when the game starts on DirectX 11. If WWMI does not draw with it there, remove Overtone under Timbre's Settings → Runtime → Setup; the next Play installs it again.

A mod that looks wrong in Overtone is worth trying in WWMI: if it looks wrong there too, the mod itself is broken (When a mod does not draw right).

#Where things are

You look for In Overtone

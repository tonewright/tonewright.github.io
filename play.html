Play with Overtone

Start Wuthering Waves with your mods, from Timbre or without it, the in-game keys and overlay, other tools beside Overtone, and removing it.

Modding breaks Kuro Games' terms of service, and the game runs anti-cheat. Play at your own risk.

#Start the game from Timbre

Press Play. Timbre installs or updates Overtone in the game's folder, then starts the game.

The first Play also downloads Microsoft's shader compiler. Afterwards Settings → Runtime → Setup says Installed and current. When it says Installed, and a newer build is waiting, the next Play installs it.

If Timbre asks Texture quality, pick the textures you downloaded in the game's launcher. It asks once; Settings → Runtime → Launch arguments holds the answer.

New to Timbre? Start with Getting started.

#Start the game without Timbre

Once installed, Overtone stays in the game's folder. Start the game as you always do, from its launcher, Steam or Epic, and Overtone loads with it and draws the same mods.

  • Overtone works only in DirectX 12, the game's default. If you switched the launcher to DirectX 11 for WWMI, switch it back.
  • The overlay works without Timbre, and what you change there is saved for Timbre to see.
  • Adding mods and updating Overtone still need Timbre.

#In-game keys

Key What it does
Home Opens and closes the overlay
F6 Hides the game's UI until pressed again
F7 Stops the game fading your character out when the camera is close
F8 Turns every mod off or on
F9 Saves a screenshot without the overlay
F10 Reloads mods after you change their files
F11 Dumps the character on screen as a mod (How to create a mod)
F12 Records the character on screen while she moves, then dumps her; press again to stop early

A mod's own keys, such as the one that takes off a hat, work only while that mod is on screen.

To change a key, use Settings → Keys in the overlay, or Settings → Runtime → Keys in Timbre.

To hide only your UID and keep the rest of the HUD, turn on Hide the UID in Settings → Drawing in the overlay, then start the game again. Other text in the same corner, such as the login status, is hidden with it.

#The overlay

Press Home. Its sections are down the left side:

Section Use it to
Wardrobe Turn outfits on and off for the character on screen, and set a worn outfit's switches, sliders and looks.
Presets Save up to eight sets of outfits on a wheel (Save what I wear here) and put one back on (Wear).
Settings Change how mods are drawn, loaded and keyed.
Tools Point at something to see what it is and dump it (Picker), or see what Overtone is doing (Report).

Outfits, mixes and presets are in Mix outfits and presets.

A setting changed in the overlay lasts until you quit the game. Press Save to keep it. Timbre's Settings → Runtime → Options holds the same settings.

#ReShade and other tools

ReShade, OptiScaler, Special K and DXVK work beside Overtone.

  1. Install the tool into the game's folder as its own guide says, as dxgi.dll or d3d12.dll.
  2. Press Play in Timbre. Overtone moves the tool's file aside and loads it after itself.

Settings → Runtime → Setup lists each tool with a switch to stop loading it.

#Remove Overtone

In Timbre, open Settings → Runtime → Setup and press Remove beside Overtone. This takes Overtone out of the game's folder and puts back any tool it moved. Your mods are untouched, but mods Timbre made native still do not load in XXMI (Add your mods).

The next Play installs Overtone again.

If something looks wrong in game, see When a mod does not draw right.

Applies to Timbre 26.1017.0 and Overtone 26.1017.0.

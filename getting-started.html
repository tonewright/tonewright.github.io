Getting started

Install Timbre, add your first mod and see it in Wuthering Waves.

Overtone draws mods inside the game. Timbre is the app where you add, turn on and edit them, and it installs and updates Overtone for you. You only download Timbre.

Modding breaks Kuro Games' terms of service, and the game runs anti-cheat. Play at your own risk.

You need:

  • Windows 10 or 11.
  • Wuthering Waves, set to DirectX 12 in its launcher (the default).
  • A mod to try: a GameBanana page link, or an archive (.zip, .rar, .7z) you downloaded.

On a Mac or Linux, Timbre manages your mods but does not play: see Timbre on a Mac or Linux.

#1Install Timbre

  1. Open the releases page and download Timbre_<version>_x64-setup.exe from the newest Timbre release.
  2. Run it. Windows may show Windows protected your PC, because the beta is not signed yet: press More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Timbre installs for your Windows user only, with no administrator rights, and opens.

#2Choose your mods folder

Timbre keeps your mods in one folder, your library.

  1. Timbre opens on No library indexed yet. Press Set the Mods folder.
  2. Check Game folder. Timbre usually finds the game; if the field is empty, choose the folder that holds Wuthering Waves.exe.
  3. Beside Suggested library, press Use. Timbre makes an empty Overtone Library folder near the game, outside its launcher's folder, with a folder named for the game inside it, and sets that as your Mods folder.

Any empty folder of your own works too: choose it as Mods folder instead. Do not pick a folder inside the game's folder. If you already use XXMI, do not pick its Mods folder: see Add your mods to bring those mods over.

#3Add a mod

  1. Press Add a mod.
  2. Paste the mod's GameBanana link and press Download, or drop the archive on Timbre's window and press Import.
  3. Leave Enable after install (or Enable after import) ticked.

The mod appears on its character's page in the library. Timbre files it by the character it changes, whatever the archive is called.

#4Play

  1. Press Play.
  2. The first time, Timbre installs Overtone into the game's folder and downloads Microsoft's shader compiler.
  3. The game starts. Go to a place where your character is on screen.

#5Check it works

  1. Press Home. Overtone's overlay opens. If nothing opens, see When a mod does not draw right.
  2. Open Wardrobe. Your mod is listed under its character, with a green dot while it is on screen.
  3. Turn it off and on with its switch to compare it with the game's own outfit.
  4. Press Home again to close the overlay.

That's it: the mod is drawn, and it stays on the next time you play.

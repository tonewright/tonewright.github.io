Timbre

26.1106.0On GitHub

Protect a shared mod with a password, so a code posted in public opens nothing without it.

New

  • Adding a big library now shows its progress on screen, phase by phase.
  • New characters appear in Timbre's grid before their game update ships.
  • Settings is redesigned: wider, with each group's title beside its rows, window colours as plates and Help on one page.
  • Health lists the mods a failed change left half moved, and Try again moves them back.

Fixed

  • A folder of many mods is filed mod by mod under each character, and folders that repeat a character's name are removed.
  • A mod shared from another version of Timbre now opens wherever it can, and where it can't, Timbre offers the update and reopens it.
  • Meshes from WWMI-Tools keep their vertex colours and UV maps, and what a mesh lacks is filled in as you save.
  • A mesh imported from another mod's model saves in Blender with no UV maps to rename.
  • Timbre says so when a change to your library fails half way and could not be put back, and when a setting does not save.
  • Models saved from Blender keep a piece's part number when Blender writes it as 3.0.

26.1106.0On GitHub

Turn a mod on while you play and it appears at once, then sharpens in moments, however large its textures.

New

  • Borrowed outfits look right on every machine: each one now carries the shader and textures it needs.

Better

  • A library imports about a fifth faster after an update.

Fixed

  • Switching mods no longer stalls the game for a moment on some switches.
  • Aemeath's mods wear their own body textures again.
  • Switching a mod on no longer takes away the part it replaces before the mod appears, so a character stays whole while it loads.
  • Closing the game with Alt+F4 no longer saves a crash dump as if it had hung.
  • Mods that set their textures from a separate ini now keep them when imported.
  • A long save or a sleeping computer no longer lets a second program write over a mod you are editing.
  • A mod with impossible sizes in its files is now refused with a reason instead of crashing the game.
  • A change the game could not apply is now said on screen and tried again, and your mods keep drawing.
  • A switch you change while the library is reloading, or a choice another program held for a moment, is no longer lost.
  • A downloaded mod can no longer touch files outside its own folder.
  • A mod whose switch starts on a position that draws nothing now starts on its first position instead of installing empty.
  • PNG textures that say they are sRGB now draw with their true dark tones instead of washed-out grey.
  • Ramielle Dan for Yangyang Xuanling draws all three outfits and its wings right, and each outfit bends as it should.

  • A mix saved as one mod now shows its textures in Timbre's preview, and Mix's bar and save are simpler.

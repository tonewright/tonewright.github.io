Timbre 26.1019.0

Fixed

Overtone MCP server 26.1019.0

Maintenance: nothing a player would notice changed.

18 October 2026

Timbre 26.1018.0

Save diagnostics now includes the game's own crash report and ReShade's log, and can include the hang dump.

New

  • Timbre's setup now shows the beta terms for you to accept before it installs.

Fixed

  • Completing a choice of several words in a condition now writes it in quotes, so the condition still reads
  • When the model view cannot draw a mod, Timbre says so and lets you close it, rather than going blank.
  • A mod whose file ends in .INI is now named in Health with the rename it needs, and one named ~DISABLED is read as WWMI reads it.
  • The texture picker and Paint name maps as modders do (Diffuse, Light map), and a Zenless Zone Zero mod's picker offers its own maps.
  • A mod's Outfit row now names the outfit it is listed under, even after the outfit it was filed under is renamed.

Overtone MCP server 26.1018.0

An AI assistant connected to Overtone can now reset a mod to what Overtone derives from its files, dropping every pick.

Fixed

  • Proposed changes and kept edits name each texture by its folder in the mod and say a material's textures in words.

Overtone runtime 26.1018.0

Mix in game shows each outfit as a column of checkboxes, keeps what you point at lit, and asks before dropping changes.

New

  • Pointing at a piece in Timbre's Mix lights it in game without slowing the game.
  • The saved diagnostics list the game's DirectX files, and the log says when the game has not used Overtone's after a minute.
  • Timbre's runtime options now list only the settings a player changes; old troubleshooting switches are gone.

Better

  • The Mix page opens faster, in game and in Timbre, most for a character with many outfits
  • In game, the Mix page lights the piece you point at for as long as you point at it, without slowing the game.

Fixed

  • A mod's normal map on hair or a face, which the game never shows, is no longer put on them.
  • Outfits that carry a bone remap no longer come out with twisted legs or bodies after an update.
  • Mods that pick their textures by WWMI's format filters, like Slap's Hsin, Suisui and Yangyang outfits, wear their textures again.
  • Rover (Male) mods now get their textures without anyone having to play him first.
  • The Mix page in game and in Timbre agree on what a part shows, and no longer call a part bare where the game's own shows.
  • With ray tracing on, a mod that changes one part of a character, such as her face, now draws.
  • Aemeath mods whose hash fix moved a light map onto the normal map draw their own light map again.
  • With ray tracing on, an accessory worn beside an outfit now draws, and the outfit with it, instead of the game's own.
  • Mods with their own UV layout no longer turn see-through in a skill form, such as Suisui's knees in her ink form.
  • Mods an earlier update left on outdated bone weights, like Hsin's Azur Bunny, are rebuilt so they no longer stretch.
  • Mods that work out their bone map size, rather than writing it as a number, no longer stretch once made native.
  • With ray tracing on, an outfit turned on while playing now shows instead of the character's own clothes.
  • Mods that bring their own copy of WWMI's bone remapper, like Slap's Hsin outfits, no longer stretch or twist once made native.
  • A key that steps through outfits is no longer dropped when one of its steps applies in every state
  • An update's conflict over a look or a switch's condition now reads with your mod's own option and choice names
  • An update no longer marks a look as changed when only the wording of its condition differs, and a look conflict reads as the file says it
  • Timbre and the in-game Mix page agree on which pieces an outfit wears, and an outfit another one covers no longer shows as worn.
  • A description written by hand reads a choice spelled as a number the same way in its looks, conditions and defaults.
  • A greyscale PNG texture with a transparent shade now keeps its transparency.
  • A texture picked for one outfit of a mod that ships several in one folder no longer lands on the others
  • Mods that build more than one skeleton, like Setsuna no Chou for Shorekeeper, are no longer refused.
  • Textures picked for a mod whose key moves several values land where that key's look shows them, and explain tells what it draws
  • A mod split over two folders that stopped updating again after its other half updated updates again.
  • Turning off Talk to Timbre during a session stops dumps and screenshots being reported to Timbre.
  • With Talk to Timbre turned off, a dump or a screenshot no longer opens a channel to Timbre.
  • A mod split across folders, such as Hsin - Morning Mist, now changes every folder when you switch an option in one.
  • Mods split into several folders that share textures and switches, such as Hsin - Morning Mist, now wear them in every folder.
  • A modded body weighted to the game's own face or hands no longer trails them by a frame in motion.
  • A modded character no longer lags a frame behind the game's own face and hands while she moves.
  • A modded part weighted to the game's own face or hands now follows them in motion instead of breaking away.
  • A mod that edits the game's shaders, or binds a vertex buffer past the fifth, now behaves in Overtone as under WWMI.
  • The overlay saves settings only when you press Save, F7 keeps its own choice, and improved defaults reach settings you never changed.
  • A mod line with a note after a ; no longer hides a part XXMI shows, and shape keys work whatever their letter case or comments.
  • Outfits that pick their textures through nested RabbitFX lists, like Hsin - Morning Mist, now wear their own textures.
  • Hsin's Glimpse and Glimpse Ult mods can now be made native instead of being refused.
  • A mod that replaces only part of a character's model, like an outfit for Hsin's ultimate form, is now recognised as hers.
  • Settings you never changed now follow Overtone's defaults, so a better default reaches you with the update.
  • A mod shows under the same name in Timbre and in the game's overlay: the name its author gave it, else its folder's.
  • The overlay and Timbre agree on preset names: Beach and beach are one preset, and the wheel never makes a second Beach.
  • A dump no longer takes in a mesh of another character that shares only unused bones with the one you picked.
  • Mods turned on after the game started with none drawn now appear without restarting the game.
  • Mixing outfits in Timbre now shows what the game draws: an outfit you mix in is never dropped for another when you wear it.
  • The game always loads the mod library Timbre shows, even from an older settings file.
  • The log, the shader cache and edited shaders are kept again when your Windows user folder has letters outside English.
17 October 2026

Timbre 26.1017.0

The library has a Weapons tab beside Characters once you have a weapon mod, and a weapon inside an outfit switches on its own.

New

  • A finished download can now be cancelled while it installs, and one interrupted mid-install is finished on the next start.
  • A weapon inside a mod pack is shown with its outfit's name and cover, and Health files a pack's weapons by name.
  • A weapon inside a mod pack gets a folder named for it, out of any empty folder it shipped in.
  • Updating a mod pack keeps each weapon inside it, with its choices, even after its folder was renamed.
  • Turning an outfit on now turns on the weapon that came with it, and you can still turn the weapon off on its own.
  • Another mod manager's backup, temp and restore folders are no longer listed as mods, and Health offers to clear them. (#4)
  • Health lists mod folders not named after their character and mod, and renames them all with one press.
  • Renaming a mod or moving it to another character renames its folder to match, and an update still finds a renamed mod.
  • A mod you import or download is named once, and Timbre files its folder as Character - Name, such as Chisa - Radiant.
  • Importing several mods shows each one's progress, and Cancel stops an import before anything is added.
  • Health can update a mod anyway past its warning, and Undo puts it back.
  • Health lists the mods an update left as they were, with why, and the update's notice opens it.
  • When you add a mod from an archive, Timbre shows the character it is for and fills in its author, link and version.

Better

  • Turning a mod on or off does less work behind the scenes, so the page settles sooner.
  • The Loadout page updates faster after you turn a mod on or off.

Fixed

  • The Play button appears as soon as Timbre finds your game, without a restart.
  • Health's Not updated list names each mod, shows its cover and opens it to say why.
  • Renaming a choice now renames it in every condition, and the description pane stops warning about switches that exist.
  • Turning a mod on or off moves its switch at once, and the mod's panel no longer flickers.
  • Overtone guesses the roles of unlabelled mod textures by one set of rules, and its library check after an update runs faster.
  • A mod deleted outside Timbre stops being drawn and leaves your presets, and turning mods on and off is quick again.
  • Several mods dropped on a mod now ask whether they belong with it, and one dropped on a character's page lands under that character.
  • Switching presets no longer prepares the same textures again each time.
  • One press of Make native now makes every mod native it can, without pressing again.
  • Mods that only hide or retexture the game's own parts are no longer listed as not native in Health.
  • Mods without a cover show their character's art in Health instead of a black square.
  • Downloading or updating a mod no longer freezes Timbre while the archive unpacks.
  • Importing a mod no longer freezes Timbre, and the dialog shows that it is importing.

Overtone MCP server 26.1017.0

Timbre asks you to update, and changes nothing, when a newer Timbre has already used your library.

Better

  • Timbre does less disk work after each mod you turn on or off, so it stays responsive while it prepares textures.

Fixed

  • Timbre and AI assistants reach a game that has just started on their first try.

Overtone runtime 26.1017.0

A character dressed by several mods, a hair and an outfit say, moves as one on every piece

New

  • A modded part weighted to a bone only the game's own face or hands carry follows it instead of freezing.
  • A mod keeps drawing right after a game update that renumbers a character's bones, near-identical bones included.
  • Nested mods are named by their own words, such as Bow or Chakram, instead of their folder's path.
  • Iuno's ring from an outfit mod wears the mod's own texture instead of the game's.
  • The in-game Wardrobe lists and groups your mods as Timbre does, one row a mod with its parts inside. (#4)
  • Weapons in packs you installed before keep your choices, picked textures and presets when they become mods of their own.
  • Aemeath's and Denia's signature weapons in mods now wear the mod's textures.
  • Weapons and props in mods like the Radiant packs now wear their own textures instead of the game's.
  • Weapon recolours in the Radiant packs for Hiyuki, Jingran, Brant, Qiuyuan and Yuanwu now show in game.
  • Weapon mods are now mods of their own, switched on and off apart from the outfit they came with.

Better

  • Modded characters cost less frame time to draw.
  • Clone skills with many afterimages, like Qingxiao's, cost far less frame time.

Fixed

  • A mod split over two folders that stopped updating after an Overtone update updates again and draws all its textures.
  • A mod you picked a texture for no longer loses it at some switch settings when Overtone updates.
  • Denia's Customization And Mega Wardrobe wears its own outfit in the open world, not its material maps.
  • Outfits whose texture files were renamed or re-saved by their author, or that turn a texture off, now show what the author shipped.
  • Mods whose textures an ini names with a leading backslash, like Denia's Remiel Alt in the overworld, now draw them.
  • A mod whose bone weights use the wide layout is paired with the game's bones correctly after a game update.
  • A weapon mod that names its own weapon is listed and switched as that weapon, even where its model matches another one.
  • Simple Hiyuki Sliders' Alt+4 puts back the switches Ctrl+Alt+4 saved, as it does in WWMI.
  • Some outfits now show their own normal map, and a few no longer wear a texture meant for another part.
  • A key that resets a mod's switches, like Ctrl+4 in Simple Hiyuki Sliders, now works in Overtone too.
  • Mods that recolour their outfits in game, like Denia's Customization And Mega Wardrobe, wear their own textures again.
  • Installing a mod whose key saves many sliders at once, like Simple Hiyuki Sliders, no longer hangs at Unpacking.
  • The game's log and saved diagnostics now name Overtone's version instead of a commit.
  • Keys that run a list kept in another of a mod's ini files now switch what that list changes.
  • Qingxiao's Cheongsam Bunny wears its own normal, light and material maps on every piece.
  • Mods whose extra ini files run shared lists, like Qingxiao - Crimson Blossom Decree, now wear the right textures on every piece.
  • Iuno's chakram ring in Moonlit Crystal no longer wears the game's shiny reflection texture as its colour.
  • Ciaccona's Dusty Bunny gun and staff wear the mod's own textures instead of her cloak, and its gun is a weapon mod of its own.
  • An outfit whose body sits in its own folder beside its weapon no longer splits apart when it updates.
  • Weapon mods that came inside an outfit are listed as their weapon's, not the character's.
  • The Mix page, in game and in Timbre, now offers every outfit the Library shows for a character. (#4)
  • A mod's keys now work beside another mod on the same character or her weapon, and a toggle key turns its part off again.
  • A texture or switch you change by hand in a mod's description now stays through updates, and the game shows it as soon as you save.
  • Hiding your UID no longer crashes the game now and then as it loads something new. (#4)
  • Mods you add or change in Timbre while the game runs show in the in-game Wardrobe the next time you open it. (#4)
  • The in-game Report's Mods cost shows a measured figure, or an estimate marked as one, instead of a made-up frame loss.
  • Accessories a mod hangs on a character's own small parts, like Roccia's glasses and sandals, now stay on her instead of floating.
  • Qingxiao's and Hiyuki's Radiant hair and eye colour options show in the normal form again instead of the second form's textures.
  • Recolours of props in the Radiant packs, such as Carlotta's rifles and Augusta's crest, now show in game.
  • Jingran's and Qingxiao's Radiant hair and eye colour options no longer show the other form's textures in the normal form.
  • Eye colour choices of Qingxiao mods now show on a first import instead of the legs texture.
  • Effect textures a mod ships for the game's shaders are no longer worn as a character's base colour, which tinted some halos and rings.
  • A weapon mod now draws in game, whether it is installed on its own or inside a character's mod.
  • The overlay says a preset was saved, worn or moved only once it was, and asks you to wear a mod before saving one.
  • A preset can no longer be saved with no mods on, and one that was is gone; a preset tells you once about a mod you removed.
  • Mods sitting side by side in one character's folder are no longer read as one mod.
15 October 2026

Timbre 26.1015.1

Report a problem in Settings saves your logs and opens a GitHub issue with your note already filled in.

New

  • Timbre's close, minimise and maximise buttons hide under the window and slide out when you rest the pointer at its top edge.
  • Timbre can turn off a feature that misbehaves in one game without a new release, and releases no longer show the flags list.
  • What's new links the issue a fix answers, and the issue is closed with the release that fixes it.
  • Report a problem can include the game's hang dump when one exists, if you tick it.
  • What's new can be narrowed to Overtone, Timbre or the MCP server, and lists everything since the Overtone you ran.
  • Timbre now offers to report a problem when the game closes after one, and has a Report button at the foot of the left bar.
  • Updates now show highlights for Overtone, Timbre and the MCP server to swipe through, and Play checks for an update first.

Fixed

  • Timbre's model view shows a Zenless Zone Zero mod whole, its hair, body and weapon together.
  • The highlight card in What's new no longer shows the next card through it.

Overtone MCP server 26.1015.1

Logs, About and diagnostics now name the real version, and a build from source shows what's new too.

Overtone runtime 26.1015.1

Hide the UID in Settings → Drawing keeps your player ID off the screen and out of screenshots, the rest of the HUD as it was.

Fixed

  • The game no longer hitches for a moment while a character's face is moving.
  • The game no longer hitches for a moment while a mod's look is first prepared.
  • The game no longer hitches for a moment when a character first comes into view.
  • Mods with a separate ini per body now switch textures on every body, and liberation forms stay put between imports.
  • The game no longer stutters for a moment when mods first load.
  • Mods with several textures for one part keep every switch they had, and more of them show their own textures.
  • More of the video memory that mods let go of now goes back to the game.
  • Mods that swap several textures for one part, or ship a near-miss sheet beside it, now show the right texture instead of none.

Timbre 26.1015.0

The texture question before Play lists SD, HD and UHD, with HD chosen; press Play to start.

New

  • Timbre looks for updates every hour, and soon after your PC wakes from sleep.
  • Play asks which textures you downloaded when the game's folder doesn't show it, and remembers your answer.

Fixed

  • A folder holding mods for several characters, added to Timbre, becomes one mod per character, each under its own character.
  • Timbre no longer fills its log with warnings before you choose a mods folder.
  • Play from Timbre no longer stops the game at start with "Use launcher to start game"; it starts with HD textures where installed.

Overtone MCP server 26.1015.0

Maintenance: nothing a player would notice changed.

Overtone runtime 26.1015.0

The game no longer crashes at start on DirectX 12 when ReShade is installed beside Overtone.

New

  • Diagnostics, in game and in Timbre, now shows how much video memory Overtone uses, slow frames and freezes.
14 October 2026

Timbre 26.1014.2

Timbre shows how far it is in preparing your mods' textures, and hides it while you play.

New

  • Settings › About says when the running game still uses an older Overtone.

Fixed

  • Saving diagnostics no longer says overtone.log is missing after the game's log was set aside.

Overtone MCP server 26.1014.2

Fixed

  • The runtime diagnostics in Timbre show the last run again after an update set its log aside.
  • A crash while a mod is saved no longer leaves a broken backup copy behind.

Overtone runtime 26.1014.2

Mods with prepared textures show the moment they are switched on or come into view, and sharpen a moment later.

New

  • Mods off screen for a minute give back most of their video memory, and sooner when memory runs short.
  • Mods worn last session whose textures are not prepared yet start loading at game start, as memory allows.
  • A texture several mods or outfits share is loaded once, and a mod's other looks are ready the moment you switch to them.
  • Overtone keeps at most three backup copies of a mod's files, and keeps an old model at an update only if you edited it in Blender.
  • Each part of a character is headed the same way in Timbre and in game, by what it is and its number, like Body 3.
  • Every mod piece shows its id beside its name, the same in Timbre and in game, and Chinese piece names read in English.

Fixed

  • A mod's eyes now show its own iris where the game's iris used to show through.
  • Changing a switch no longer makes a mod's switch list in the overlay blink when the mod has switches for two forms.
  • Free versions of mods that leave out a key, like Shiroho's Radiant trials, show in game again, their choice moved from Timbre.
  • Colour switches of mods like Radiant Qingxiao now change her hair, eyes and outfit in game.
  • The game closes two seconds sooner and no longer reports an error that never happened.
  • Mods that fade a dress or top with their own see-through shader, as Hsin - Rouged Lips does, now draw it see-through.
  • Characters wearing a mod now teach Overtone their textures as you play, like characters without one.
  • The in-game overlay keeps working after the game restarts its graphics, and the old graphics memory is let go.
  • The Diagnostics page shows what mods cost even when it is under one frame a second.
  • If reloading your mods ever fails, the game now reads them once more rather than leaving new ones unloaded.
  • A texture no longer stays blurry after you change outfits or mods while it was still loading.
  • A Carlotta mod's crystal hair no longer draws over her normal hair.

Timbre 26.1014.1

When a Timbre update is ready, the Play button turns into Update.

New

  • Updating Timbre now shows what is new first, and Settings shows every product's version.
  • Each release on GitHub now lists what is new, better and fixed.

Fixed

  • Timbre suggests a mods folder outside the game launcher's folder and uses a library you already have instead of making a new one.

Overtone MCP server 26.1014.1

Fixed

  • An AI client connected to Overtone no longer leaves gigabytes of memory held after checking or borrowing mods.

Overtone runtime 26.1014.1

Mods fixed for a newer game version by a fixer tool place their textures from what the fixer's backups say.

New

  • Overtone remembers the texture names a game update replaces, so mods made before the update keep finding their textures.
  • The log names what the game was compiling when it stalls, and a hung game writes its dump.
  • When the game freezes, Overtone saves a file showing where it stopped, so the freeze can be found and fixed.
  • Overtone learns the game's textures even from characters no mod dresses, so more mods find theirs.

Better

  • Characters seen for the first time in a session no longer stall the frame while their shaders are read.
  • Overtone reads a character's textures in a fraction of a second, where it took two or three.
  • Overtone no longer reloads your mods each time it learns one of the game's textures.
  • A slider moved in game, or a switch flipped in Timbre, now takes effect without reloading every mod.

Fixed

  • Changing outfits or mods often no longer slowly uses up graphics memory or makes characters take longer to appear.
  • Mods on Zani's stockinged outfit now shade her skin and hands with their own textures in the character menu.
  • Mods keep the textures Overtone placed for them when a game update or a new texture table arrives.
  • Logs and diagnostics no longer contain your Windows user name.
  • Switching a character's form no longer reports her textures as missing.
  • A slider no longer snaps back while you drag it.

Timbre

26.1123.0On GitHub

Fixed

Overtone

26.1123.0On GitHub

Aemeath's, Hiyuki's and Denia's looks have names you can read, such as Pink and Frost; the old names still work

New

  • An imported mod now replaces more of a character's glow and effect textures, where the game drew its own before.
  • A WWMI mod that finds its textures by their picture now draws them where its author placed them.
  • You can hide your coordinates on screen, as your UID is, with Hide coordinates in Settings → Privacy.
  • The overlay's Debug page now shows only in developer mode, turned on by clicking Timbre's version seven times.
  • Your UID is hidden from the first frame by default; turn off Hide UID in Settings → Privacy to show it.
  • Hiyuki's black stocking finish and similar effects in other mods now draw, as they do in WWMI.
  • A button of a mod's own menu that sets several pieces at once is now a look you can wear from the Wardrobe and Timbre.
  • A stocking finish a mod draws in its own shaders now follows the stockings you pick.
  • Qingxiao - Crimson Blossom Decree's Liberation outfit now appears during her Liberation, so its second set of toggles works

Fixed

  • Denia's expressions are worn again, and her Expressions page lists each one she can wear.
  • Fixes a rare crash as the game closes.
  • Fixes a rare crash as characters first appear after loading.
  • Removing Overtone now also takes its settings file out of the game's folder
  • Mod pieces Overtone redraws keep every setting of the game's own drawing, so none is drawn with the wrong shape or blend.
  • Overtone gives back memory it no longer uses, and Tools → Report shows how much RAM it holds.
  • A transformed form an earlier install left undrawn is drawn after Timbre next starts, with no install again.
  • A mod with a normal and a transformed form draws both forms once installed; the transformed one drew nothing.
  • Hsin's Spare Set + Yukata draws without its moving skirt and sleeves instead of with its skirt stretched to the floor.
  • A see-through lace or other masked piece no longer cuts holes into the rest of the outfit or the body.
  • A see-through piece set by a single number no longer discolours or hides what is behind it.
  • A see-through piece on a part that keeps the game's own textures no longer discolours or hides what is behind it.
  • The game no longer crashes when its display format, window or graphics device changes after you opened the overlay.
  • A mod's glow mask now replaces the game's on more of its parts.
  • Suisui - Night Enchantress Alt no longer wears an effect texture as a ramp on her outfit.
  • Textures some mods bind for another look no longer draw as sparkle, ramps or face shadows on Suisui, Mornye and others.
  • A mod missing some of its textures no longer draws those parts black where the game's own texture fits them.
  • A body-size slider you write with no starting value now starts the model at its own size, not a tenth of it.
  • A slider you give keys but no speed in its description now moves while you hold its key.
  • A mod made before the last game update no longer draws a skin paler or with the wrong texture over its shading.
  • A modded character seen from far away is drawn whole or as the game's, never with a piece of the mod missing.
  • A mod that draws water or skin in its own shaders no longer crashes the game with a lost graphics device.

Timbre

26.1120.0On GitHub

Picking XXMI's Mods folder now brings its mods into your library, and mods you already have are not added twice.

New

  • A mod you share now arrives with the patterns its own shader makes, and Inside lists them under Renders
  • Make an expression of your own from scratch on the Expressions page: find her shape keys, see each on her model, and save it.
  • A GameBanana mod behind a password now asks for it on its Downloads card.
  • A download that failed or was paused can now be removed from Downloads.

Fixed

  • Adding your mods no longer waits forever on a game whose mods will not read, and the log says which mod a slow import is on.
  • Renaming a switch in Timbre keeps what your loadouts saved on it, and Undo puts it back.
  • When one download Overtone needs fails, the others still arrive, and Play names the one that didn't.
  • The Expressions page and Mix stand her in the middle of the stage, not small and to one side when her outfit holds a weapon.
  • Face on the Expressions page comes in to her face when she wears a mix, and Mix lights a mix's face and hair as hers.
  • The Expressions page shows her in the one outfit you chose on her page, without her weapon or her other skins.
  • Timbre's button for a character's expressions now reads Expressions, under a theatre masks icon.
  • A download that fails now says why in your language.
  • A GameBanana mod whose title is only its character's name now installs instead of failing.
  • Timbre's pull counter picks up your Convene History link while the game runs, instead of waiting until it closes.
  • Updating a mod always offers Replace, even when GameBanana doesn't list what's inside the file.

26.1120.0On GitHub

Deep Sea Witch's droplet densities, and other effects a mod's own menu sets, can be picked in Timbre and in game.

New

  • A mod that draws its own texture with a shader now uses the textures its outfit wears under your switches, as in WWMI.
  • Mods that animate their own mesh with a shader of their own, such as pulsing tentacles, now move in game.
  • Water, wet skin and other effects whose mod reads a data file as a buffer now draw.
  • After a game update, a mod's transformation look shows from your first transformation, with no wait for an Overtone update.
  • A mod drawn in its own shader shows sooner in later sessions, its look prepared as the character loads.
  • A mod that mixes its own textures by its switches, like Origin Toggle's patterns, now shows each toggle's look
  • The Face page shows the expression on her model as you pick it, framed on her face, with Whole body one click away.
  • Timbre has a Face page for each character: wear, change and save her expressions, and see each change on her in game.
  • A character with no face mod keeps her expressions after a game update.
  • A mod's face keeps its expressions after a game update renumbers the game's own face keys.
  • A mod's sheer stockings drawn over the legs show in game instead of being left out.
  • Outfit mods that change a character's face now show her tongue too.
  • The Face page has nine new expressions, among them Bedroom eyes, Side eye and a giggle, a gasp and a moan that move.
  • A new expression, Tongue tip, rests her tongue's tip on her lower lip with her mouth half open.
  • Faces can move now: Lick lips, Chewing, Blown kiss, Slow wink and Rolling eyes play in motion, and Panting breathes
  • A mod's key now works only with exactly the modifiers it shows, so Ctrl+5 no longer moves a switch bound to 5.
  • A mod key bound with Ctrl+Alt now moves only its own switch, not the ones bound to Ctrl or Alt alone.

Better

  • After you add or update a mod, Timbre and the game no longer recheck the mods it shares textures with every time they start.
  • Fewer Overtone updates make mods with hidden or see-through parts wait for their first draws again.
  • A mod is made ready again in about half the time after a change, so a switch or an update reaches the game sooner.

Fixed

  • A mod holding two forms of a character no longer fills the log with a bone warning while it draws right.
  • Water a mod sets by its own menu, such as Deep Sea Witch's droplets, now shows in game.
  • A piece a mod draws through a helper ini's list, at a range that list hands on, now shows.
  • A mod's face no longer takes another character's expression when that character wears an outfit mod.
  • The overlay no longer lists a character as on screen while she is out of sight behind the scene.
  • Pieces a mod draws on both sides, like open cups and sleeves, no longer show their inside from one side.
  • A mod with 16-bit weights kept in XXMI's folder no longer draws as spikes; the game's outfit shows until it is native.
  • Suoming's Deep Sea Witch wet skin and Hsin's Indigo Oath outfit now draw with their own shaders.
  • A mod's face no longer takes another character's expression when the two share a party.
  • Names you gave a mod's switches and your loadouts' choices stay when its menu switches pieces in other folders
  • A mod that ships the game's own texture beside its edit of it now wears the edit.
  • Converted mods that named the wrong texture for a light or highlight map draw like in WWMI, such as Denia's Bunny Bunny Puff.
  • After a game update, a mod's transformation look no longer stays on once the transformation ends.
  • Characters changed by the game's last update draw your mods right from the first launch, before you play them.
  • A mod whose menu switches pieces in other folders now shows one switch per choice, and keeps the choice you had made
  • When the game loses the graphics device, Overtone's log says where the game saved its own crash dump.
  • Effects a mod draws through a shared effects pack, such as body water, now follow that mod's own keys.
  • Sanhua's tongue out is full again, not a narrow point, and no character's tongue out pushes into her lips
  • Expressions and tongues now show on mods that draw her face from a second copy of her model, such as Suoming's Yukata.
  • Shorekeeper, or anyone wearing one outfit's face over another outfit's body, now wears expressions.
  • The expressions page reads Expressions in game and in Timbre, in every language Timbre offers.
  • Mods now draw on PCs where another program loads Windows' own graphics files into the game before Overtone.
  • Yangyang: Xuanling mods made before her update draw every part of her again.
  • A mod made for an older game version no longer teaches Overtone wrong textures that other mods of that character then pick up.
  • A texture a mod's ini marks sRGB or linear, or one saved with a 1/2.2 gamma, now shows the colours it shows in WWMI.
  • The game no longer crashes at launch when another program loaded Windows' DirectX files before Overtone.
  • Water effects ported onto a mod, as on Hiyuki's Mist of Morning Glory, now draw and turn on and off by their keys.
  • Effects drawn by a mod's own shader that reads the game's texture coordinates one by one now show instead of being skipped.
  • The game no longer crashes now and then as Overtone prepares a mod's shader.
  • A mod whose shape keys start each batch by another value, such as Sigrika's Evening Gown, loads again.
  • The game no longer says DirectX 12 is not supported when Disable fullscreen optimizations is ticked on it.
  • A crash from a stack overflow now leaves a line in overtone.log saying where it happened.
  • OptiScaler loads beside Overtone on its own, with its own folder and settings, and no longer stops the game past the menu.
  • A face mixed from one outfit onto another's body now moves with her expressions.
  • Lili's tongue no longer curls up like a horn when she sticks it out.
  • A switch that only changes a mod's shader, such as a glow, now shows in the overlay and in Timbre.
  • A body mod that reshapes itself in the game, like Alice's Bunny Girl suit, now comes in instead of being set aside.
  • A mod that paints its patterns with a shader of its own, such as Lily's Origin Toggle, now loads, without the patterns for now.
  • Hsin's Mature Woman keeps her glasses on her face and her jacket on her body.

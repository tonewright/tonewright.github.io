A character's forms

The name of each character's forms for a Figure condition, what each changes, and how to give forms a name of your own.

Reference4 min readTimbre 26.1106.0 · Overtone 26.1106.0

#What a form is

A form is a state the game puts a character in for a while, such as her Resonance Liberation, in which it draws other textures on the same meshes. Figure is <name> in a condition (Conditions) holds while she is in that form, and the sheets you give for it replace the ones she wears the rest of the time.

A character's whole model can change too, as in her Liberation. The game's list calls that a figure. A piece or a sheet held to one draws while she is in it.

#The names a form has

Name What it is
The game's own, such as DownNew1 Read from the game's files. Overtone and Timbre write this one back, unless the condition holds in every form that shares a short name.
Form and six lowercase hexadecimal digits, such as Form592973 The form's number. Every form has one, and it is the same on every machine.
A short name, such as Liberation Given by an Overtone update once it is known what the form is. Several forms may share one, and Figure is Liberation then holds in any of them. Overtone and Timbre write it in place of the forms' own names when a condition holds in all of them.

A name Overtone used for a form that was merged or renamed still reads as that form, and is written as the form's name the next time Overtone or Timbre writes the condition; see Names that still work.

A row whose Changes cell reads "as" a form is the textures her model wears on those parts when that form is off. It matters for a character who arrives in a form, as Denia does in DownNew, so a sheet for her other look has a form to be worn under. For a character who arrives in her own look, the row does nothing: a sheet under it never draws.

Denia's, Lucilla's and Rebecca's whole-model figure is called Liberation, and a form is never given a figure's name. A character not in the table has no form shipped yet.

#Give forms a name of your own

A short name comes with an Overtone update. For a group of your own, give a condition a name under [when] and use it wherever a condition goes:

[when]
cast = 'Figure in [DownNew1, Xianren]'

[[rule]]
when   = 'cast'
wear.1 = { diffuse = "Textures/Hair_Cast.dds" }

The name belongs to that one mod.

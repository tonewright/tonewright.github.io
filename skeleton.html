The Wuthering Waves skeleton

What a Wuthering Waves character's skeleton looks like, which of its bones are the same on every character, and what catches authors out when weighting.

Explanation5 min readTimbre 26.1106.0 · Overtone 26.1106.0

For anyone weighting a mesh in Blender, new to the game's rig or coming from another game's. It answers:

You want to know Go to
How she stands and how the bones hang What it looks like
Which bones you can count on What every character shares
Where to put weights Which bones hold the skin
Why your weights came out wrong What catches authors out

You see this skeleton as Rig in a mod's Blender scene. It has four kinds of bone:

Kind Example What it does
Main bone thigh.L The frame of the body: spine, arms, legs
Twist forearm twist 2.L Turns with a limb along its length, so the skin does not pinch
Helper knee back.L A small bone beside a joint, which holds the skin in shape as it bends
Chain hair 3.2.L A line of bones that swings, here chain 3 and its second bone. The face's small bones are chains too

Bone names says how each name is built.

#What it looks like
Pose Standing, arms down and out about 55° below level, legs straight
Facing She looks along Blender's -Y
Her left +X, so .L is on your right as she faces you
Ground Her feet stand on Z 0
Size Metres. Her head bone stands 1.0 to 1.5 m up, by body type

The body hangs from hips, and everything else hangs from the body:

hips
├─ spine 1 ─ spine 2 ─ chest
│                      ├─ neck ─ head ─ hair, face
│                      └─ clavicle ─ upper arm ─ forearm ─ hand ─ five fingers
│                                    └ twists    ├ twists
│                                                └ elbow front, back
├─ hip front, mid, back
└─ thigh ─ calf ─ foot ─ toe
   └ twists  ├ twist
             └ knee front, back

Skirts, cloth and hair chains hang from whichever bone they start at, so they are spread over this tree. To see what a bone moves, pose Rig: it changes nothing in the mod.

#What every character shares

Group How many Across characters
Main bones, in Body 22 One name, one bone on everyone. Only Buling has no hand
Fingers 30, 15 a hand One name, one bone on everyone
Twists, in Body Usually 16, from 12 to 35 The same limb on everyone. The number can mean another place
Helpers, in Body Usually 14, from 12 to 20 One name, one bone
Hair About 60, up to 157 Her own
Skirt About 45, up to 141 Her own
Cloth About 40, up to 117 Her own
Face Usually a few, up to 45 Her own

Most characters are one of eight body types. Two characters of one type have their main bones in one place, so a mesh weighted on one moves the same on the other.

Weights on main bones go straight across to another character. Twists and chains do not, as their numbers are each character's: bring the mesh in with its skeleton and Overtone pairs them (What keeps the weights it came with). On another body type the mesh keeps its own proportions.

#Which bones hold the skin

No single bone holds a limb. These are the game's own weights, on a typical character:

Limb Share of its skin
Thigh thigh twist 1 45%, thigh 25%, thigh twist 2 25%
Calf calf 80%, calf twist 1 20%
Upper arm upper arm twist 1 30%, upper arm twist 2 20%, upper arm 20%, upper arm twist 3 15%
Forearm forearm 40%, forearm twist 2 35%, forearm twist 1 20%

Characters differ a lot around these shares. Each helper holds under 1% of the body's skin on most characters, so a joint bends on its limb bones.

Do this: let a mesh that lies on the body take its weights from the skin under it, which brings these shares with it (When to paint weights yourself). Paint by hand what hangs away from the body.

#What catches authors out

The trap Why Do this
A chain's name means another bone on another character Each character's chains are numbered for her Bring the mesh in with its skeleton (What keeps the weights it came with)
A twist you expect is not there Some characters have no thigh twist 1 or calf twist 1, and a forearm has from 1 to 13 twists. Rig also hides a bone no vertex weighs in Unused Read the numbers off Rig, Unused included, not off another character
A twist sits on top of its limb The first twist of a thigh or an arm usually starts on the limb's own joint, so the two overlap Pick it in the outliner
Skin on a hip helper lags the leg hip front, hip mid and hip back hang from hips. In game they turn only part of the way with the leg, and in a Blender pose not at all Weight the top of the thigh on thigh and its twists
A hair or skirt mesh does not swing Only chain bones swing, and copied weights take the skin nearest the mesh Paint it on chain bones of Hair, Skirt or Cloth
One character has two skeletons Hsin, Jinhsi, Rover and Cartethyia have a second one, for a swimsuit or a second form. Main bones keep their names, chains do not Weight it on the Rig of the model you have open
A name opens with ~, or reads c3.b17 The mod was made for an older game version, or the table does not hold the character yet Marks
A number finds the wrong bone A number means a bone only inside one Part collection, and a bone can sit in several Use the name (WWMI numbers)
Weights vanish on save The game reads four or eight weights a vertex Find the weights a save cut
A save refuses a piece One piece reaches at most 256 bones, or it is weighted to a bone you added When a save is not taken
A bone you renamed has its old name back Each time you open the mod, its bones take the game's names Leave them (When a name changes)

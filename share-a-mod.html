Share a mod

Send a mod to a friend from Timbre as a picture or a code, add one you are sent, and see how it is kept private.

How-to7 min readTimbre 26.1028.0 · Overtone 26.1028.0

Share sends a mod straight from your Timbre to your friend's:

  • Nothing to upload. You post a picture or a code anywhere you like: a chat, a forum, a message.
  • Only what the game needs. It sends the mod's files and the pictures Timbre shows for it. Your Blender file, Paint layers and backups stay with you.
  • You decide how long. The share ends when its time is up, or when you press Stop sharing.
  • One press to add. Your friend pastes it into Timbre and adds the mod.

#Before you start

  • The mod is in your library and is native (What native means). Timbre makes mods native as they land. If the panel says Make this mod native to share it., look under Health.
  • Timbre stays open while your friend adds the mod. It sends the files from your computer.

#Your first share

About a minute.

  1. Open the mod's page and press Share. A panel opens at its side.
  2. Leave the choices as they are.

  1. Press Share in the panel. Getting it ready shows, then a picture of the mod with a square code on it.
  2. Press Copy picture and paste it into a chat or a post. Or press Copy code and paste the text.

Your friend pastes it into Timbre (Add a mod someone shared).

#What the picture and the code are

  • They are the same key. The picture holds the code as a square code and as hidden text. Either one opens the share, even if a site re-saves the picture and drops the hidden text.
  • Whoever has one can add the mod. Post it where you want your friends to see it, and no wider.
  • Your Timbre sends the files itself. It does so while it is open. Nothing is stored anywhere else. A day or a week share carries on after you restart Timbre.

#Choose how it is shared

Choice What it does
Lasts A day, A week or Until I quit. A day is the default. The panel says when it ends: Until {when}, while Timbre is open or Until you quit Timbre. On a computer that keeps no secrets safe, only Until I quit is offered, and it ends within a week.
Your switches On by default: your friend gets the mod set as you wear it.
Direct connection Off by default. Faster, but you and your friend see each other's IP address. It works only if your friend turns it on too.

#Add a mod someone shared

  1. Copy the picture or the code, then press Ctrl+V in Timbre's window (⌘V on a Mac). To add a saved picture, drop it on the window.
  2. Timbre opens Add a mod and says Getting it from the sharer. It then shows the mod's name, character, cover and files.

  1. Press Import. The mod arrives in Downloads and appears in your library.

Direct connection is in the same window, off by default. Leave it off unless you trust the sharer with your IP address.

If you already have the same files, the button reads Wear this setup. It puts the sharer's switches on the mod you hold and turns it on. Undo takes it back.

If the sharer's Timbre is closed, step 2 says The sharer's Timbre isn't answering. It has to be open. Ask them to open it. If it closes after you press Import, the mod waits in Downloads as Waiting for the sharer. It tries again by itself, even after you restart.

#Send a mod as a file

To send a mod by any other way, press Export in the panel and save the file. Your friend drops it on Timbre's window like any archive (From an archive or a folder). It has no time limit.

#Stop sharing

Press Share on the mod again, then Stop sharing. People who already added the mod keep it. Anyone who has not can no longer add it. While Getting it ready shows, Cancel does the same.

If you change the mod after you share it, your friends still get the copy you shared. Press Stop sharing, then Share again, to send the new one.

#What you can't do yet

You try What happens
Share an outfit Not yet. Share each mod.
Share a mod that is not native, or only hides or changes the game's own parts Make this mod native to share it.
Share a mod that uses another mod's files This mod uses files from another mod's folder. Share can't send those yet.
Share a texture you painted over a PNG or JPEG A texture you painted over is a PNG or JPEG. Share can't send that yet.
Share a very large mod Over 2048 files or 4 GiB, it fails with an error. Send it as a file instead.
Send your edits Your friend gets the finished mod, not your Blender file or Paint layers.
Show who shared it Your friend sees the mod's name, author and cover, as Timbre shows them to you. Nothing says who sent it.
See every share at once Each mod's own panel holds its Stop sharing.

#For the curious: how it works, and whether it is end-to-end encrypted

How it works.

  1. Share copies only the files the game draws, and up to nine of the mod's pictures stripped of who made them and where, into a private folder and writes a list of them, with a fingerprint of each file. It signs the list with a key made for this one share.
  2. The code holds your Timbre's public key, the public key that signed the list, a secret, the end time, whether a direct connection is allowed, and the name of a relay. It never holds your address.
  3. Your friend's Timbre connects to yours through the relay, a post office that passes sealed envelopes between two Timbres. The relay is run by n0, the makers of the iroh connection library, not by us. The connection is encrypted with TLS 1.3, between the two Timbres' keys.
  4. Your friend's Timbre proves it holds the secret, takes the signed list, then each file, and checks every file against its fingerprint. A file that was changed on the way is refused.
  5. No program or script travels with the mod. A mod with its own shader sends the shader's source, which compiles when you wear it, as with any downloaded mod.

Is it end-to-end encrypted? Yes, in transit. The ends are the two Timbres that hold the code. The files are encrypted on your computer and decrypted only on your friend's, so the relay and the network cannot read them. A stranger cannot pose as the code you posted, because the code contains your key. We rely on iroh's encryption and have not audited it ourselves.

What it does not cover:

Limit What it means
The code is the key. Whoever has the picture or the code can add the mod. A picture posted in public is a public share until it ends.
The relay sees who and when. It sees both IP addresses, the time and the size, not the files. With Direct connection on at both ends, each of you also sees the other's address.
Files are not encrypted on disk. Your private folder and your friend's library hold ordinary files.
A signature is not a review. It proves the files came from the key in the code and arrived whole. It does not say the mod is good.

Applies to Timbre 26.1028.0 and Overtone 26.1028.0.

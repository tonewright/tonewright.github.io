Guides/Play with mods

Give a character an expression

Choose a character's expression in game: the overlay's **Expressions** page and the face wheel, Timbre's **Expressions** page, the expressions Overtone ships, strength, faces of your own, a mod's, her tongue.

How-to14 min readTimbre 26.1123.0 · Overtone 26.1123.0

Give any character a smile, a wink or a pout while you play, with no face mod made for each one. A face mod is a mod that replaces a character's face, often as part of an outfit.

  • Dozens of expressions for every character. One list, worn on her own face or a face mod's.
  • She still blinks. The game goes on closing her eyes as she blinks, over the expression.
  • Faces that move. Some drift to another face now and then; others play a few faces in turn, such as a blown kiss.
  • One press in game. Pick from the overlay, or hold a key and point at one of eight on a wheel.

#Before you start

  • The game runs with Overtone (Play with Overtone).

#Your first expression

About a minute, in game.

  1. Press Home. The overlay opens.
  2. Open Wardrobe. It shows the character on screen; Q and E step to another.
  3. Click Expressions, the third page of Wardrobe. The page lists the game's under Built in, yours above them.
  4. Click Smile. She eases into it in game, and the line under her name says what she wears.
  5. Click Smile again. She goes back to her own face, and the line says Her own face.

#How an expression sits on her face

  • An expression is a whole face. It sets her brows, eyes, where she looks, her mouth and, in some, her tongue. What it leaves alone stays the game's.
  • Strength is hers, not the expression's. Strength, under the lists, sets how far she wears it, from 0 to 100%; it starts at 80%. Wear another expression and she keeps the strength.
  • What she wears is kept. Your library holds it as soon as you pick it, with no Save: she wears it the next time you play.
  • The page lists only what her face can wear. A few expressions need a shape some faces lack, such as puffed cheeks, so two characters may see different lists; Timbre lists the rest under She can't wear. What a character wears is hers alone.

#Pick from the game's expressions

Overtone ships these. The page shows them under Built in, the kinds mixed; Timbre marks the moods and moving faces:

Kind Expressions While worn
Still faces Smile, Grin, Wink, Smug, Surprised, Angry, Sad, Content, Bored, Scared, Crossed eyes, Sultry, Laugh, Blissful, Dazed, Blank, Shocked, Scream, Tearful, Worried, Puppy eyes, Embarrassed, Pout, Disgusted, Gritted teeth, Determined, Suspicious, Kiss, Yawn, Asleep, Cat smile, Puffed cheeks, Puzzled, Bedroom eyes, Shy, Wince, Whistle She holds it.
Tongue faces Ahegao, Tongue out, Bleh, Lick left, Lick right, Tongue tip, Cheeky She holds it, her tongue out (Her tongue).
Moods Cheerful, Flirty, Drowsy, Nervous, Naughty, Hungry, Taunting, Side eye At random moments she drifts to another face, holds it a moment and comes back. Each mood keeps its own pace: Taunting flicks to a lick several times a second, the others every several seconds.
Moving faces Panting, Lick lips, Chewing, Blown kiss, Slow wink, Rolling eyes, Moaning, Gasp, Giggle She plays through a few faces in turn, starting from the face she has. Lick lips, Blown kiss, Slow wink and Gasp play once and stay on their last face; the others repeat.

#Change one part of her face

Under Piece by piece is a row for each part her face can change: Brows, Eyes, Gaze, Mouth and, where Overtone can move her tongue, Tongue. Step a row with the arrows beside its choice, or point at it and press Left or Right. Each row offers the worn expression's own for that part, as As Smile while she wears Smile, then Her own, which gives the part back to the game, then these, where her face has them:

Row Choices
Brows Happy, Raised, One raised, Flat, Sad, Angry
Eyes Smiling, Half closed, Closed, Wink, Wide, Angry
Gaze Up, Down, Left, Right, Crossed, Small pupils
Mouth Smile, Smirk, Laugh, Open, Round, Angry
Tongue Out, Down, Up, Left, Right

The line under her name then says the expression is changed, or Wears your own mix where none is worn. A changed row lasts until you quit the game, or until you wear an expression or press the worn one again, which clears every changed row. To keep them, save them.

#Save a face of your own

  1. Change one or more rows under Piece by piece. Save turns on.
  2. Press Save, or Ctrl+S. A field opens with a name filled in.
  3. Type a name.
  4. Press Enter. She wears it, and it joins Yours, at the top of the page.
The name Under the field
Blank Give it a name.
One the game, a mod drawn now or one of yours already uses, whatever its capitals Smile is taken. Pick another name., with the name you typed

Cancel or Esc closes the field. A face of yours is offered on every character whose face can wear it.

To remove one, point at it under Yours and press Delete. It leaves every character who wore it, every loadout and the face wheel.

#Edit her expressions in Timbre

  1. On her page in Timbre, click Expressions. Her expressions are listed under Yours, From and each mod's name, and Built in, with a Mood or Moves mark on those that move.
  2. Click an expression. Her model beside the list shows it, framed on her face; Whole body shows all of her, and Face frames her face again. While the game runs she shows it in game too, and the page says Shown on Shorekeeper in game.
  3. Press Wear. A check marks it in the list, and Strength under On her sets how far she wears it.
  4. Change a row under Piece by piece, or a shape under Shape keys, by the game's name for it, such as E_Close_L. The page says Shown on Shorekeeper in game as you change it.
  5. Press Save. One of yours is saved in place. A game's or a mod's asks for a Name, filled with My face.
  6. Type a name and press Save again. It joins Yours, and she wears it.

Add a shape key sets one it leaves alone. Take off gives her own face back. Kept in lists what holds it. Delete removes one of yours after asking.

A change you haven't saved is kept while Timbre runs. Click another expression to compare: a dot after the changed one's name marks it, and clicking it again shows the change on her model. In game she shows only the expression you are looking at, and wears what you saved. Discard lets the change go. Quitting Timbre asks first, and Save and quit keeps every changed expression, a game's or a mod's under a new name, without changing what she wears.

Timbre judges her face before the game does:

Case Result What to do
Her face lacks a shape it needs Listed under She can't wear; click it for why Pick another, or wear her own face for a tongue face
A face mod that reshapes her face The game may still refuse one the page offers Read her face's line in the log (Where to look)
No game folder set in Timbre Every expression is listed as wearable, and her model holds still Set it in Settings → Folders

Her model is her as she wears the outfit chosen on her page: the mods you have on her there, at the switches you set, without her weapon. With none on, it is a dump of her made with her mods off and close to the camera (Dump the character). When it can't show a face, it says why:

Case Her model What to do
You have mods on for several of her outfits The outfit chosen on her page Choose another outfit on her page, then click Expressions
No mod on her and no dump of her No face to show here yet Turn on a mod that dresses her, or dump her in game
The mods on her leave her face to the game Her model holds still Turn on her face mod, or see the expression in game
Her face mod was made before a game update, and no dump of her since the update is in your library Her face at rest, and This mod's face was made for another version of the game, so its expressions can't be shown on it here. Dump her in game; the dump names its shapes
An expression she can't wear Her face at rest, and She can't wear it, so her face is shown at rest. Pick another
A tongue face, on a face mod with no dump of her in your library Her model shows it without her tongue Dump her in game

#Make a new expression in Timbre

  1. On her Expressions page, click New expression, at the top of Yours. Her model shows her own face, and Add a shape key opens under Shape keys.
  2. Type part of a shape's name in Search shape keys, or click a part of her face, Brows to Tongue.
  3. Point at a shape. Her model shows it at full until you point away from the list.
  4. Click it. It joins Shape keys at 50%, and her model wears it; while the game runs, she does too.
  5. Drag its slider. Her model follows.
  6. Press Save. A Name field opens, filled with My face.
  7. Type a name and press Save again. It joins Yours, and she wears it.

Click a lit shape in the list again to take it out. Piece by piece works here as on any expression. Save and Discard show once the face moves something; Discard starts again from her own face. The new expression is kept as any change is (Edit her expressions in Timbre).

#Use the face wheel

The face wheel puts eight expressions round a ring over the game, for the character on screen.

  1. In the overlay, open Settings → Keys and set Face wheel (In-game keys).
  2. Press Save 1 change.
  3. In game, hold that key. The wheel shows.
  4. Point at a slot. She wears its expression as you point: at a quarter strength next to the middle, rising to full at the rim.
  5. Let go. She keeps that expression at that strength.
You let go She
On a slot Keeps its expression at the strength you pointed at
In the middle, once you have pointed away from it Takes her expression off
Anywhere else, or on a slot whose expression her face can't wear Stays as she was

A mood does not drift while you hold it.

The slots start as the first eight expressions: Smile, Grin, Wink, Smug, Surprised, Angry, Sad and Content. To change one, point at an expression on the overlay's Expressions page and press a number key, 1 to 8: it goes into that slot. The slots are the same for every character.

#Wear an expression a mod ships

A mod's author can give her outfit expressions of its own. While that mod is drawn on her, they are listed under From and the mod's name, between Yours and Built in, and one with a game expression's name takes its place on her. Turn the mod off and they leave the list; one she wore gives way to her own face until the mod is worn again.

Case What happens
You saved an expression of that name Yours is worn; the mod's is not listed.
Two drawn mods give her one name The first drawn keeps it; the log names both.
The mod names no object, or another mod over the same parts is drawn first None listed.
Her face lacks a key it names Not listed.
Overtone cannot move her tongue (Her tongue) Its tongue keys are left out; an expression of tongue keys alone is not listed.
A mood drifts, or a sequence steps, to an expression nothing holds The mood never drifts there; the sequence is not listed.

To write your own, see Give her an expression.

#Keep a face in a loadout

A loadout keeps the expression each character wears when you save it, and puts it back when you wear it (Loadouts). The face wheel's slots stay as they are.

#Her tongue

The game's faces hold a tongue but never move it. Overtone works out how to move it from the shape of each face, the game's own or a face mod's, and a tongue face opens her mouth as far as the tongue needs.

Her face What you get What to do
Her own face, or a face mod that keeps her mouth The Tongue row and every tongue face Nothing.
A face mod that reshaped her mouth Overtone finds the mouth in the mod's own shape and moves the tongue there Nothing.
A face mod whose mouth Overtone cannot find, such as one that cut it away No Tongue row. Tongue out, Bleh, Lick left, Lick right and Lick lips are not listed; other tongue faces and moods are worn without the tongue Pick a face without a tongue, or wear her own face for a tongue face.
Her face, her own or a mod's, after a game update that changed it The Tongue row and every tongue face, once Overtone has matched her face (After a game update) Nothing.
The same, where Overtone could not match the shapes that open her mouth No Tongue row. Tongue out, Bleh, Lick left, Lick right and Lick lips are not listed; other tongue faces and moods are worn without the tongue Update Overtone when Timbre offers it, or install the mod's update once its author makes one.
A face whose tongue lies in a part of it the game never draws The Tongue row and the tongue faces are listed, and no tongue shows Pick a face without a tongue.

#After a game update

An update can change a character's face. Overtone matches what it can by how each shape of her face moves.

Case What happens What to do
She wears a face mod Overtone matches the mod's face to the new one, so she keeps her expressions. Until it has seen her face in game, the page says No expressions for her yet. An expression needing a shape it could not match is left out. Install the mod's update once its author makes one (Update a mod).
No face mod Overtone matches her own face to the new one the first time each game session it sees her with the overlay's Expressions page or the face wheel open, or while she wears an expression. Until then the page says No expressions for her yet and A game update changed her face. Her expressions come back with the next Overtone update. An expression needing a shape it could not match is left out. Open the overlay's Expressions page while she is on screen.
No face mod, and those words stay while she is on screen with the overlay's Expressions page open Overtone cannot find her face. Update Overtone when Timbre offers it; the next Play installs it.
No face mod, and the page says Overtone cannot read her face's shape keys yet. Overtone found her face and could not match it. The same.
A character new to the game The page says Overtone cannot read her face's shape keys yet. The same.

#Keys on the Expressions page in game

Key What it does
Q, E The character before or after
Arrow keys Move through the expressions and rows; on a row, Left and Right step it, and on Strength move it by 5%
Space, Enter Wear the expression; on the one she wears, take it off
1 to 8 Put the expression in that slot of the face wheel
Ctrl+S Save what she wears under a name
Delete Take her expression off; on one of yours, remove it
Ctrl+PgDn The next section, Loadouts

Rename a face of yours There is no rename yet.
Find a shape in Timbre by what it does Shapes go by the game's names, such as M_Laugh; point at one to see it on her model.
Undo a face change in History History takes back mods only.
See her mood drift on her model in Timbre Her model holds the mood's own face; the drifts come only in game.
Hide a weapon inside one of the mods you mixed on her Her model shows the weapon with that mod.
See all of her in a skin no dump of yours shows Her model shows only the piece holding her face; dump her in that skin to see her whole.
Save a mood or a moving face of your own A face you save holds still.
Wear, from a loadout, an expression her face can't wear She keeps her own face, and the log says the expression is not worn and which shape her face lacks (Where to look).
Keep her tongue clear of her lips while they purse, close or move aside On some faces her tongue passes through her lips.
Keep her tongue out with her mouth shut A tongue face always opens her mouth.
Have her eyes follow the camera Her gaze is the expression's or the game's.
Keep her expression from far away Far from the camera she goes back to the game's own face.
Wear an expression on her own face with ray tracing on The page says Faces are off with ray tracing.
Wear one over a face mod with no shapes of its own The page says Her mod's face has none.
